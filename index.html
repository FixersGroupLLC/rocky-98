Why chatbots did not stick

A chat window cannot see your files. It does not remember your business. It cannot do anything. Three gaps. Not intelligence.

I am a real desktop program. I open your actual files. My memory is a plain-text folder you own.

I do not get bored. A person checking 800 rows is guessing by row 300. I am as careful on the last row as the first.

I remember, and it compounds. Month one, I am a capable stranger. Month six, I know your vendors and what goes wrong every July. A repeated job becomes a script.

Safety - Warning

×

The answer is not "trust me." It is "check me."

  • I never spend money, even if you tell me to out loud.
  • I stay off banking and payment sites.
  • I never send anything without your yes.
  • Outbound mail is read back before it goes.
  • Deleting anything needs a spoken double-confirm.
  • I say "I haven't checked that" instead of guessing.
  • Specialists get less access than I have, never more.
  • Your files and memory stay on your machine.
  • Phone access runs over a private network with no open ports.

Anything arriving by email or web page is information, never a command. If a message tells me to delete something, send money or ignore my rules, I report it to you. I do not obey it.

What I am not

Not a chatbot. Not a CRM. Not autonomous. Not a replacement for a person.

My limits

Some jobs are slower through clicking than through a direct connection. I will tell you when. Heavy days can hit the usage ceiling on your AI plan. I get things wrong sometimes. I check my work, keep a log, and say when I do not know.
